**Two halves. One solution.**

Split is a 2D pixel art puzzle platformer where you split into two independently controllable clones to overcome obstacles, solve puzzles and reunite to escape.

Play the original SPLIT game mode or the new game mode: The Ascent: Guide Blue and Orange up towering levels, use their matching climbing surfaces, and work together to reach the top.

### The Ascent
Take the puzzle upward! Explore 4 climbing levels, including a climbing tutorial. Blue and Orange can climb surfaces matching their colors, while special surfaces can only be climbed when reunited.

Combine climbing, careful timing, and teamwork to reach the peak.

## Features

- Splitting and reuniting mechanics
- Two controllable clones
- Four increasingly challenging levels
- Color-coded barriers, pressure plates and climbable surfaces
- New game mode: The Ascent
- Timed switches and doors
- Interactive tutorial
- Hand-drawn pixel art graphics
- Main menu and ending screen
- Character animations and sound effects
- Background music

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| A / D | Move left / right |
| SPACE | Jump |
| F | Split / Reunite |
| TAB | Switch between clones |
| Hold E + W / S | Climb up / down on a matching surface |
| Hold E + A / D | Move sideways while climbing |
| R | Restart level |
| ESC | Pause |

## How to Play

1. Split yourself into blue and orange clones.
2. Switch between them to navigate obstacles.
3. Activate pressure plates and timed switches.
4. Overcome the puzzles.
5. Reunite and reach the exit.


### Climbing in The Ascent

- Blue climbs blue surfaces and Orange climbs orange surfaces.
- Hold E to grab a climbing surface, then use W/S or up/down arrows to move vertically and A/D or left/right arrows to move sideways.
- Switch characters while holding onto a surface to let the other clone move.
- Reunite to use special climbing surfaces reserved for the main character.
- Use pressure plates and timed doors to help both halves reach the top.
